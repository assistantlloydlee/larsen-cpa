About Christopher Larsen CPA

Christopher Larsen CPA is a Los Angeles accounting practice with two areas of specialization: real estate accounting and personal tax preparation.

Christopher D. Larsen, CPA

Christopher D. Larsen is a Certified Public Accountant licensed in California. Prior to founding Christopher Larsen CPA, he held positions with EY and Deloitte. His practice focuses on lease accounting, CAM and operating expense review, and individual taxation.

Christopher Larsen CPA does not publish client names or testimonials. Client identities are held in confidence, and no engagement outcome is disclosed without written consent.

Approach

  • Personal Service. Every engagement is performed by Mr. Larsen directly. The client's point of contact is the accountant doing the work.
  • Documentary Method. Findings are drawn from the lease and the underlying records. Where the documents do not support a conclusion, the finding states so.
  • Written Findings. Analysis is delivered in writing with supporting reasoning and schedules, suitable for use in discussions with the landlord or its accountants.

Where to Begin

Most engagements begin with a CAM and lease compliance review or an individual tax return. Each service page describes the scope of work and the documents required.
