Send a Message

Sending delivers your enquiry straight to Christopher Larsen CPA through this site's own form. Your message is emailed to the practice and no third-party form service is used, so you do not need to open your own email program. Please do not include confidential documents. Secure transfer instructions come with the reply.

Helpful Information to Include

  • Real Estate Reviews. The property address, the leased premises, and the reconciliation period.
  • Business Accounting. Recent bank and credit card statements, the prior year return, and the current accounting file, if available.
  • Lease and Statements. Whether the lease and the most recent CAM or operating expense statement are available.
  • Tax Returns. The tax years involved and the types of income to be reported.
  • Purpose. The reason for the inquiry and the decision under consideration.
