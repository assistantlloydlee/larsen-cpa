Real estate and small business accounting, and personal tax preparation

Christopher Larsen CPA is a Los Angeles accounting practice serving commercial tenants, property owners, small businesses, and individuals. Services include landlord operating expense reconciliation review, lease accounting, small business bookkeeping and tax, and individual income tax return preparation.

Real Estate Accounting

The principal service is CAM and lease compliance review: an examination of landlord billings against the terms of the lease, with each unsupported charge documented in writing.

Related Services

  • Lease abstraction and key financial terms
  • Base year and expense stop review
  • Operating expense budget review
  • Lease accounting under ASC 842
  • Bookkeeping and financial reporting for rental properties

Begin With the Documents on Hand

For a lease review, please provide the lease and the most recent reconciliation statement. For business accounting, please provide recent bank statements and the prior year return. For personal tax preparation, please provide the prior year return and current income documents. Each service page lists the documents to have ready. The scope and fee of every engagement are confirmed in writing before work begins.
