Other Services

General Accounting Services

Accounting services for small businesses and individuals beyond real estate accounting and personal tax preparation.

Services Provided

Each engagement is scoped to the needs of the business.

  1. Bookkeeping and Monthly Reconciliations

    Monthly journal entries, bank and credit card reconciliations, and a reliable month-end close.

  2. Financial Statement Preparation

    Income statements, balance sheets, and statements of cash flows prepared from client records.

  3. Business Tax Returns

    Partnerships, S corporations, and limited liability companies.

  4. Payroll and Form 1099 Coordination

    Coordination of payroll processing and year-end Forms 1099 with the client's payroll provider.

  5. Entity Selection and Formation Guidance

    Evaluation of LLC, S corporation, and partnership structures before formation.

  6. Budgeting, Forecasting, and Cash Flow Analysis

    Forward-looking financial analysis to support pending business decisions.

  7. Accounting System Implementation and Cleanup

    QuickBooks and Xero setup, and remediation of existing files.

  8. Special Projects

    Financial analysis engagements scoped to a specific question.
