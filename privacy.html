Privacy

Privacy Policy

This policy describes the information this website collects and how it is used.

Information Collected

The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. It loads no third-party fonts or resources, so viewing these pages discloses the visit only to the host that serves them.

Contact Form and Email

If the contact form is used, the fields completed (name, email address, phone number if provided, company if provided, and the message) are sent to Christopher Larsen CPA. The form is relayed to the mailbox through a third-party form delivery service, which processes the submission in transit. Messages sent by email arrive at that mailbox directly.

The information is used to answer the inquiry and to carry out work requested. It is not sold, rented, or used for marketing lists. It is kept for as long as it is needed for those purposes and for the period professional and legal requirements call for, and is then removed.

Confidential Documents

Please do not send leases, reconciliations, tax records, or other confidential records through the contact form. State what is available, and Christopher Larsen CPA will arrange a secure way to transfer it.

Requests Regarding Personal Information

To ask what information is held, to correct it, or to ask for it to be deleted, write to connect@larsen-cpa.com. Christopher Larsen CPA will respond within a reasonable period and will explain if a legal or professional obligation prevents immediate deletion.
