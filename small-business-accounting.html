Small Business Accounting

Small Business Accounting and Tax

Accurate books and correctly filed returns for owner-operated businesses, prepared by a California CPA who is also your point of contact.

Who This Is For

Owner-operated businesses, professional practices, restaurants and retailers, real estate investors, and other small businesses with an LLC, S corporation, partnership, or sole proprietorship.

Services Provided

  1. Bookkeeping and Monthly Close

    Transaction coding, bank and credit card reconciliations, and a reliable month-end close.

  2. Financial Statements

    Income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement prepared from your records.

  3. Business Tax Returns

    Federal and California returns for partnerships (Form 1065 / CA 565), S corporations (Form 1120-S / CA 100S), LLCs (CA 568), and sole proprietors (Schedule C).

  4. Payroll, Sales Tax, and 1099 Coordination

    Coordination with your payroll provider, sales tax filings, and year-end Forms 1099.

  5. Accounting System Setup and Cleanup

    QuickBooks and Xero implementation and remediation of existing files.

How It Works

  1. 01

    Initial Conversation and Document Review

    A short conversation and a look at your current records.

  2. 02

    Written Scope and Fee

    The scope and fee are confirmed in writing before work begins.

  3. 03

    Onboarding of Accounts and Records

    Accounts, statements, and accounting files are brought in and organized.

  4. 04

    Close and Returns

    Monthly or quarterly close, with returns prepared at year end.

To Begin

Please provide recent bank and credit card statements, the prior year tax return, and your current accounting file if you have one. The scope and fee are confirmed in writing before work begins.
